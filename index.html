How CollabFlow works

Every listing on the platform passes through the same review step before anyone sees it.

For brands

1

Contact CollabFlow and share your campaign requirements.

2

Our team reviews and adds the campaign to the platform.

3

The campaign is approved and published to Brand Hub.

4

Matching creators discover and apply to it.

5

CollabFlow coordinates the collaboration.

For creators

1

Contact CollabFlow and share your rates and details.

2

Our team reviews and approves your creator profile.

3

Your profile is published to Creator Hub.

4

You browse brand opportunities that fit your niche.

5

CollabFlow coordinates the collaboration.
