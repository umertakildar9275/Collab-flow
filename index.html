How it works

Two sides, one coordinated workflow

For brands

  1. Create your brand account and share your campaign requirements
  2. CollabFlow reviews and approves your campaign
  3. Campaign is published to the Brand Hub
  4. Matching creators apply through the platform
  5. CollabFlow coordinates the collaboration

For creators

  1. Create your creator account and share your details and rates
  2. CollabFlow reviews and approves your profile
  3. Profile is published to the Creator Hub
  4. You view and apply to suitable campaigns
  5. CollabFlow coordinates the collaboration

About CollabFlow

One team coordinating both sides of the deal

CollabFlow is an agency-managed platform, not an open marketplace. Every brand and every creator is reviewed before anything goes public, and every collaboration is coordinated by our team from first message to delivery.

For brands: a reviewed pool of creators, without managing outreach yourself.

For creators: real campaign briefs, with rates and requirements agreed up front.
