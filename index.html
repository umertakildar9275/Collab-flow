How CollabFlow works

One managed workflow, two sides of the table. Our team reviews every listing before it goes live.

FOR BRANDS

  1. Contact CollabFlow with your campaign idea
  2. Share campaign requirements and budget
  3. CollabFlow reviews and approves the campaign
  4. Suitable creators are connected to your brief
  5. Collaboration happens, coordinated end to end

FOR CREATORS

  1. Contact CollabFlow to get listed
  2. Share your details, categories and rates
  3. CollabFlow reviews and approves your profile
  4. View brand opportunities suited to you
  5. Apply and collaborate, coordinated end to end

Collaboration guidelines

General expectations for anyone using CollabFlow. Full policies live in the footer.

Provide accurate brand, creator and campaign information.

State campaign requirements and deliverables clearly upfront.

Respect the content requirements both sides agree to.

Discuss and confirm payment terms before work begins.

Meet agreed content deadlines, or communicate early if that changes.

Keep all communication professional and on-platform where possible.

CollabFlow reviews and approves listings before they go public.

Detailed policies are available on our Terms and Guidelines pages.

These guidelines are general and don't constitute legal terms. See the full Terms of Service and Collaboration Rules for details.

About CollabFlow

CollabFlow is a managed collaboration platform, not a self-serve marketplace. Every brand campaign and every creator profile is reviewed by our team before it becomes public — so both sides work from information that's been checked, not just submitted.

B

For brands

Reach creators who already fit your category and budget, without sorting through unqualified inbound.

C

For creators
