ABOUT COLLABFLOW

A managed layer between brands and creators

CollabFlow is an agency-run platform: every campaign and every creator profile is reviewed before it becomes public, and our team coordinates the handoff once a match happens.

For brands

Share your campaign requirements once. We review and publish them to the right audience of creators, and coordinate applications as they come in.

For creators

Share your rates and portfolio once. Approved profiles surface relevant brand opportunities without exposing your private contact details publicly.

Why managed

A reviewed listing is a trustworthy listing. CollabFlow checks each side before it goes live, so both brands and creators know who they're dealing with.
